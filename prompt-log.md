## Stage 3 Reflection

ChatGPT  was especially helpful.  While using Claude, after creating my hypothesis,  my worksheet was not really working in a way that allowed me to answer the questions being asked.  I felt like it was making me recreate the Farm Profit Lab.  Using ChatGPT was helpful because I could create my project in a systematic way, with ChatGPT providing feedback on areas that needed clarification or reworking. This helped me understand the economic concepts and encouraged me to do my own work.  This project was  enlightening because I did not have experience working with AI on complex problems.  As this  class moves at a fast pace,  ChatGPT helped reinforce the economic concepts highlighted in perfect competition.

While AI assisted with some of the steps in creating my project, I used several methods to verify the processes and results rather than simply accepting the AI output. These included manually checking the q=1 tomato calculation, comparing my model with the Farm Lab, comparing the model’s season profit with the Farm Lab’s season profit, using Solver, checking binding and slack constraints, testing additional beds, using Excel to check for formula errors, and making sure the result did not depend on the Solver starting point.
